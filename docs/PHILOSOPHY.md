# WARENA philosophy

**Status:** LIVE orientation document · 2026-10-26

## Freedom Is a System

Freedom here is not a slogan about infinite features. It is the practical ability to:

- know what is running on a machine;
- repair or replace components without a closed vendor ritual;
- keep working when the wide-area network fails;
- hand the system to another competent person with documentation.

## Against theatre

WARENA rejects:

- fake dashboards and invented uptime metrics;
- “AI product” catalogues that hide the operator;
- security claims without stated practice;
- presenting research notebooks as shipped products.

## For grounded work

WARENA prefers:

- GNU/Linux and inspectable tooling;
- written scope before irreversible changes;
- configurations that match real power, hardware, and skills;
- Kenya/East Africa operating conditions taken seriously (cost, bandwidth, hardware diversity).

## Authorship

The human remains responsible. Automation may assist; it does not replace accountability.
