# WARENA — Orientation

**Domain:** Systems engineering · reproducibility · infrastructure  
**Public path:** NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL · customer enquiries and commercial agreements through NAKEBUSEN  
**Author:** Felix Lwova · Don Feloh · @donfelohke

**Maturity baseline:** DOCUMENTATION + operational practice per evidenced engagement; not a product SaaS

## Purpose

Enable people and organisations to run **understandable, maintainable, inspectable and recoverable** computing — including GNU/Linux, networks, reproducible environments and local-first setups — under the commercial umbrella of NAKEBUSEN.

## Portfolio relationship

- **NAKEBUSEN:** parent enterprise and only commercial interface — **Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.**
- **DIGITAL · WARENA:** NAKEBUSEN's customer-facing technology path.
- **WARENA:** systems engineering and technical enablement; not a competing checkout brand.
- **GENJE ZA WARENA:** distinct cultural and creative identity — not a WARENA product.
- **IKAI:** reflective-compute research; not a released product.

See [WARENA Portfolio Alignment (2026-10-31)](portfolio-alignment-2026-10-31.md) and the [FELOH Corpus portfolio decision](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/docs/portfolio-decision-2026-10-31.md).

## Audience

- Small offices, schools, labs, cybercafés and individuals.
- Future operators who must recover a system without tribal knowledge.
- Technical collaborators who need inspectable assumptions and handover records.

## Out of scope

- Consumer checkout brand competing with NAKEBUSEN.
- IKAI product claims or GENJE creative ownership.
- Guaranteed uptime without a written agreement.
- Claims of production deployment without host, configuration, observation and recovery evidence.

## Documentation contract

Record system purpose, hardware assumptions, operating system and versions, configuration, dependencies, threat model, backup/recovery process, validation commands, observed results, limitations and handover steps. Mark plans as CONCEPT or RESEARCH until implementation is evidenced.
