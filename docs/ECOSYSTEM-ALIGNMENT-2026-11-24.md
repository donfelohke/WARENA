# WARENA — Ecosystem Alignment Update

**Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Domain:** GRIT / CAPABILITY · **Status:** Research and engineering documentation; implementation status remains per individual record.

## Identity and purpose

WARENA is the CAPABILITY practice authored by Don Feloh: libre-first systems engineering, GNU/Linux, reproducible environments, infrastructure and technical enablement. Its purpose is to make computing understandable, inspectable, maintainable, recoverable and locally controllable.

**Warena Advanced Reproducible Extensible Networked Artefacts** is the current owner-defined technical expansion. Treat it as a creator-defined acronym, not as a claim about historical linguistic derivation. The separate Sheng/cultural naming context belongs in the FELOH Corpus naming research.

**Freedom Is a System.**  
**Build locally. Verify independently. Federate freely. Govern collectively.**

## Engineering principles

1. Human authority and explicit operator approval.
2. Libre-first software, open standards and interoperability.
3. Local-first operation, privacy and offline resilience.
4. Reproducible environments with recorded sources and versions.
5. Inspectability, repairability, migration and recoverability.
6. Reuse and attribution before recreating existing work.
7. Honest maturity labels: research, proposal, prototype, configured, tested, deployed and independently reproduced.

## Technology boundaries

The existing Technology Constitution and stack register remain authoritative. GNU/Guix and Guile are foundational preferences, not a claim that every component is GNU software. Native-first and inspectable interfaces are preferred. JavaScript/web application runtimes are not approved WARENA/IKAI application architecture; static documentation and publication remain separate concerns.

Do not treat discussed components—OpenWrt, GNUnet, IPFS, Bonfire, Pubstrate, Tor, GNU Taler, M-Pesa integrations, Odoo/ERPNext, Jellyfin/Kodi, Kiwix or solar/battery systems—as deployed merely because they appear in strategy notes. Record each component's actual state, version, host, configuration, test evidence and owner.

## Safety and operations

Treat boot, disk, encryption, routing, firewall, networking and system upgrades as potentially disruptive. Require backups, recovery paths, explicit operator awareness and recorded validation. Never publish secrets, private keys, customer data or sensitive host/network details. Do not claim successful recovery, reproducibility, compatibility or deployment without observed evidence.

## Current status and next work

This update aligns shared language only. It does not certify a deployed WARENA stack or change system code. Keep the capability map, technology constitution, stack register, desktop architecture, runbooks and validation records mutually consistent; add observed command output and test evidence when available.
