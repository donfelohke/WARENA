**Domain:** GRIT / CAPABILITY  
**Status:** Strategy and engineering identity; individual capabilities require implementation evidence.

## Identity

# WARENA
## WARENA POWERED

**Freedom Is a System.**  
**Build locally. Verify independently. Federate freely. Govern collectively.**

WARENA is the technical capability associated with NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL. It explores libre-first systems engineering, GNU/Linux, networking, infrastructure, reproducible environments and local-first computing.

The owner-defined expansion **Warena Advanced Reproducible Extensible Networked Artefacts** is a technical naming construction. It is not a claim about the historical etymology of the word.

## Role in the brand

NAKEBUSEN is the enterprise and sole customer-facing commercial transaction interface. WARENA contributes distinct technical capability where a project is scoped, feasible and agreed. “WARENA POWERED” should appear only when the relevant technical contribution is real and explainable.

GENJE ZA WARENA describes a creative and enabling relationship. GENJE remains creative practice, not a WARENA product or subsidiary. IKAI retains ownership of its reflective-computing research questions and participant safeguards.

## Purpose

Develop technical systems that people can understand, inspect, maintain, recover and govern, reducing unnecessary dependence on opaque services and fragile infrastructure.

## Engineering commitments

- Libre-first software, open standards and interoperability.
- Local-first operation, privacy and offline resilience.
- Reproducible environments with recorded sources and versions.
- Inspectability, repairability, migration and recovery.
- Human authority over consequential actions.
- Reuse, attribution and transparent dependencies.
- Honest reporting of research, prototype, tested and deployed states.

## Public About copy

WARENA is the technical capability behind NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL. Its work explores GNU/Linux, free software, networking, infrastructure and reproducible computing. It believes technology should be understandable, maintainable and governable by the people who depend on it. WARENA builds locally, documents decisions and verifies systems rather than treating technological independence as a slogan.

## Claim discipline

Do not describe a discussed stack component as deployed because it appears in a strategy document. For every public capability claim, record the repository, component/version, configuration, host or test environment where appropriate, validation method, observed result, limitations and date. Do not claim “secure,” “sovereign,” “reproducible,” “offline-first” or “production-ready” without defining and testing the property being claimed.

## Commercial boundary

Potential work may include GNU/Linux administration, workstation diagnostics and recovery, small-office networking, local-first infrastructure and technical guidance. These are potential engagement areas, not guaranteed availability. NAKEBUSEN must confirm feasibility, scope, deliverables, price, support and acceptance before an engagement is represented as booked.
