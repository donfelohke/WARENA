# Asset provenance and brand boundaries — 2026-11-24

WARENA's role in the shared brand system is technical capability and infrastructure. It is not the NAKEBUSEN salon, a booking surface, nor the owner of GENJE's creative works or IKAI research.

## Shared brand relationship

- NAKEBUSEN: customer-facing business and sole transaction point.
- WARENA: infrastructure, systems, reproducibility and technical capability.
- GENJE: creative expression, sound, livecoding, movement and choreography.
- IKAI: reflexive-computing research.
- FELOH Corpus: shared canon, evidence and governance.
- Gallery: asset archive, provenance, rights, consent and approval.

“NAKEBUSEN · WARENA POWERED” is an endorsement relationship, not a merger or a claim that every NAKEBUSEN service is technically delivered by WARENA.

## Visual and technical asset controls

The reported Library inventory contains 48 filenames (40 model-generated / 8 original uploads by metadata classification). It is provisional and does not establish file integrity, licensing, authorship, consent, visual approval or technical implementation.

Technical diagrams must distinguish VERIFIED IMPLEMENTATION, TESTED PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH, CONCEPT and UNKNOWN. An illustration of distributed systems, GNU/Linux, networking or local-first infrastructure is not proof that the depicted architecture has been deployed.

Do not convert campaign artwork into architecture evidence. Do not publish private or customer imagery as technical documentation. Refer detailed image metadata to the Gallery register.

## WARENA design language

Support the restrained editorial system without turning every asset into a technology poster: bone #F2EFEA, dark jungle green #033500, terracotta #E2725B, muted cyan #3A6D8C and matte black #111111. Prefer inspectable diagrams, clear labels, source links and explicit maturity status over decorative circuitry.

## Review gate

Every asset that makes a WARENA capability claim must have:
- repository or source reference;
- owner and contributor attribution;
- status and version;
- reproducible evidence or an explicit research/concept label;
- licence and redistribution status;
- review date and limitations.

No code, runtime, deployment or website change is included in this documentation update.
