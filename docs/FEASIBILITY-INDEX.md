## How to add a study

1. Use explicit maturity labels (RESEARCH, PROTOTYPE, LIVE, UNKNOWN).
2. Separate technical feasibility from market readiness and regulatory clarity.
3. List acceptance criteria and open questions.
4. Do not upgrade maturity without inspectable evidence (host, config, date, result, recovery).
