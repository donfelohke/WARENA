## Boundary with GENJE

**GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE** is an authored cultural and creative identity. It is not a WARENA product, systems division, service catalogue or separate checkout brand.

GENJE concerns Genge-rooted creative practice, AfroTech, livecoding, movement and choreohacking. WARENA concerns systems engineering, reproducibility, GNU/Linux, local-first infrastructure and technical operations. Their relationship is creative and infrastructural, not an assertion that one is the other.
