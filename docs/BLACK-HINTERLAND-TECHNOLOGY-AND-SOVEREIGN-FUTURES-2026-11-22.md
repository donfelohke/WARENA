# Black Hinterland — Technology, Infrastructure and Sovereign Futures

**WARENA / CAPABILITY research note · 22 November 2026 · Status: RESEARCH**  
**Canonical framework:** [FELOH Corpus — BLACK HINTERLAND: BEFORE AFRICA](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/docs/BLACK-HINTERLAND-BEFORE-AFRICA-MASTER-DOSSIER-2026-11-22.md)

## Scope

WARENA studies the material, technical and institutional dimensions of African histories and futures: how particular societies produced, maintained, governed and exchanged technologies and infrastructure, and what contemporary capabilities are required for technological sovereignty. This is not a claim that one unified African technology stack existed.

## Historical research questions

- Water, soil, seed, food storage, pastoral mobility and ecological infrastructure.
- Ceramics, metallurgy, textiles, architecture, shipbuilding, transport and toolmaking.
- Scripts, manuscripts, accounting, oral archives, calendars, navigation and information networks.
- Markets, ports, caravan routes, river corridors, trade institutions and finance.
- Councils, courts, guilds, public works, collective labour, land and resource governance.
- Local invention, borrowing, adaptation, coercion, unequal exchange and knowledge transfer.

For each case record the actual material, method, labour, energy, maintenance, ownership, access, failure modes, environmental conditions and evidence. Do not retrofit modern computing concepts onto historical systems or describe every historical practice as sustainable or egalitarian.

## Contemporary capability and futures

Research energy access and grids; computing and communications; local data infrastructure; open hardware; semiconductor and electronics supply chains; machine tools and manufacturing; agricultural and medical technology; satellite and earth-observation capacity; transport and logistics; repairability; technical education; standards; public procurement; finance and research institutions.

Distinguish:
- **Observed:** measured current capability.
- **Active:** funded or underway project with dated evidence.
- **Forecast:** modelled projection and assumptions.
- **Scenario:** plausible future, not prediction.
- **Aspiration:** declared policy goal.
- **Speculative:** imaginative proposition, not a feasibility claim.

Agenda 2063's Second Ten-Year Implementation Plan (2024–2033) is a policy framework, not proof of delivery. The African Development Bank's November 2026 outlook and ITU Facts and Figures 2026 provide dated macroeconomic and connectivity baselines; always retain their definitions and reference periods.

## WARENA disposition

Apply WARENA's existing libre-first, local-first, reproducible and human-governed engineering principles as contemporary design criteria. Historical analogy is a question generator, not evidence of direct continuity. Reuse and credit existing work before inventing new systems. Do not claim deployment, production, local ownership or industrial capacity without specific evidence.
